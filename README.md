# Paolo Troia

Data analyst working on web analytics and machine learning.

I spent twelve years in media agencies (GroupM, Publicis, PHD), going from ad operations to GA4 and Google Tag Manager implementation, consent management and reporting. After a master's in AI & Data Analytics at the University of Milano-Bicocca, I moved from collecting data to analysing it.

## Projects

**[Trenord App Reviews](https://trenord-review-analysis.streamlit.app)**  
What users complain about in the Trenord train app, from 6,951 Italian Google Play reviews grouped into topics with BERTopic and checked against a blind manual coding. Ratings fell from 3.54 stars in 2015 to 1.52 in 2025, and the most common complaint is inaccurate train information. The code and notebooks are public in [trenord-reviews-bertopic](https://github.com/paolotroia-datavizlab/trenord-reviews-bertopic).

**[Seeing Analytics](https://seeinganalytics.com)**  
A site that explains web analytics metrics with interactive visuals and real data. The queries and notebooks behind it are public in
[seeing-analytics-data](https://github.com/paolotroia-datavizlab/seeing-analytics-data).
